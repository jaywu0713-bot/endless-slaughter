ENDLESS SLAUGHTER
English v1 — Build your deck. Slay monsters.

⚔️ Combat: Turn-based card battles. Draw 4 cards and regain 2 energy each turn. Monsters have a defense pool — attacks drain defense before HP. Enemy intents (Attack/Defense/Skill) are shown each turn.

✨ Starting Blessing: Pick one of three — a random relic, a random rune, or 200 gold + a Blank Card (an unplayable curse).

🗺️ Route: Choose your room on the map each floor. ⚔️Battle · ⭐Elite · ❓Mystery · 🛒Shop · 🏨Inn · 💀Boss. Each room connects to at most 2 rooms on the next floor — plan your route!

🏨 Inn: Guaranteed right before each boss. Choose one: Rest (heal 25%) / Train (+5 ATK/DEF/Max HP) / Meditate (+5 levels).

🛒 Shop: Buy cards, stats, relics and rune fusion. Stats get more expensive each purchase; prices rise with floor. 2 random relics are stocked per visit.

📈 Leveling: Level-ups only raise max HP (+5) and do not heal. Inn rests and events are your main sources of healing.

🏆 Goal: Defeat the final boss "The Chosen of Four Gods" on floor 100! Press ` to open the admin panel.
